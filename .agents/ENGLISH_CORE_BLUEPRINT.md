## 3. Literature Chapter Blueprint (The 3-Tab Gold Standard)

Every literature chapter (Prose, Poetry, or Play) adheres to the standard 3-tab architecture tailored for English language excellence:

### Tab 1: Overview & Critical Appreciation (Reference)
1. **About the Author / Poet**: Brief, engaging context of the writer, era, and historical backdrop.
2. **Comprehensive Chapter / Poem Summary**:
   - For Prose: Chronological, engaging, high-retention storyline.
   - For Poetry: Stanza-by-stanza line analysis with deep emotional and philosophical meaning.
3. **Character Sketches & Profiles**: In-depth psychological and thematic profiles of all key characters (e.g., Grandmother in *Portrait of a Lady*, Aram & Mourad in *Beautiful White Horse*).
4. **Poetic & Literary Devices Master Table**:
   - Crucial for scoring 4/4 marks in extract-based questions!
   - Table columns: `Device Name` | `Line / Example from Text` | `Literary Significance`.
   - Covers: *Metaphor, Simile, Personification, Alliteration, Onomatopoeia, Transferred Epithet, Oxymoron, Irony, Hyperbole, Imagery, Symbolism*.
5. **Vocabulary & Glossary**: Challenging words from NCERT text with contextual meanings and example usage.
6. **Chronological Story Flow / Mindmap**: Sleek sequence diagram showing major milestones of the plot.

---

### Tab 2: Board Exam Solutions (Exact Pattern)
Divided into clean, high-contrast sections following exact board exam word limits and marking criteria:

1. **Section A: Extract-Based Objective Questions (1 Mark Each)**:
   - 3 to 4 extracted stanzas / prose passages followed by 4 questions each (Context, Poetic Device, Vocabulary, Inference).
2. **Section B: Short Answer Questions (3 Marks | 40-50 Words)**:
   - 10 to 12 high-probability questions directly assessing inference, critical thinking, and character actions.
   - Point-wise, high-impact phrasing.
3. **Section C: Long Answer / Theme-Based Questions (5-6 Marks | 120-150 Words)**:
   - 4 to 5 in-depth analytical questions (theme, character comparison, moral conflict, real-life connection).
   - Structured in 3 tiers: *Introduction -> Core Thematic Analysis -> Concluding Takeaway*.
4. **Section D: NCERT Official Textbook Exercise Solutions**:
   - Complete coverage of *Understanding the Text* and *Talking About the Text*.

---

### Tab 3: Smart Interactive Quiz (20 MCQs)
1. **Tiered Question Distribution**:
   - **Tier 1 (Q1 to Q8)**: Author/poet facts, character relationships, and key events.
   - **Tier 2 (Q9 to Q15)**: Vocabulary, lines context, and identifying poetic devices.
   - **Tier 3 (Q16 to Q20)**: Analytical, thematic, and inference-based MCQs.
2. **Smart Feedback & Dashboard**:
   - Green border + checkmark on correct answer (explanation hidden).
   - Red border + shake animation + cross on wrong answer (opens theme-colored explanation panel showing correct option and exact textual reason).
   - Ultra HD Gamified Result Dashboard at quiz completion.

## 4. Writing Skills & Grammar Hub Blueprint

Writing Skills modules are built as ready-to-use exam toolkits:

1. **Exact Board Marking Scheme Card**: Shows students exactly where marks are won or lost (e.g., *Format: 1M, Content: 2M, Expression & Grammar: 1M*).
2. **Golden Rules & Master Templates**:
   - Clean, copyable structure with fill-in-the-blank placeholders.
   - Notice Box Format, Letter to Editor Format, Job Application & Resume Layout, Poster Grid.
3. **3 Solved Board-Standard Examples**:
   - Example 1: Standard / High-Frequency Board Problem.
   - Example 2: Creative / Social Problem.
   - Example 3: Value-Oriented / Critical Thinking Topic.
4. **Grammar Editing / Error Correction Master Rules**:
   - Cheat-sheet of the 6 Golden Grammar Rules tested in boards: Determiners, Subject-Verb Agreement, Tenses, Modals, Prepositions, Conjunctions.
   - 10 Solved Error Correction Passages with *Incorrect Word -> Correct Word* tables.
