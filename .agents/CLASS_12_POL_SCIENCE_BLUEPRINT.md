# Class 12 Political Science — Master Blueprint & Gold Standards

This document serves as the permanent, authoritative blueprint for **Class 12 Political Science** across all 15 chapters. Whenever working on Class 12 Political Science, ALWAYS follow these specifications strictly.

---

## 1. Core Architecture & 3-Tab Standards

Every chapter in Class 12 Political Science MUST follow the dedicated 3-Tab structure:
- **Tab 1: Reference (Overview)** — Ultra Gold Deep Reference Textbook standard (Pradeep's / Trueman's / B.L. Fadia / Subhash Kashyap quality).
- **Tab 2: Solutions (Q & A)** — Targeted Board Questions tailored strictly according to the official Board Marks Weightage (2M, 4M, 6M, 7M).
- **Tab 3: MCQs** — 15 to 20 smart interactive questions with tiered difficulty (Easy $\to$ Moderate $\to$ Advance), live Green/Red feedback, and gamified result dashboard.

---

## 2. Tab 1: Ultra Gold Deep Reference Theory Standards

1. **Deep Conceptual Explanations First (Prose-First Architecture)**:
   - Never start topics with short bullet points alone.
   - Ultra-deep conceptual explanations detailing geopolitical background, constitutional context, historical origins, diplomatic treaties, and ideological mechanisms.
   - Content me bilkul koi kami nahi honi chahiye — full textbook depth with zero shortcuts.
2. **100% Exact Official Syllabus Headings**:
   - Use official syllabus headings word-for-word as main `<h2>` section titles.
   - Organize every section into Roman-numbered subtopics `(i), (ii), (iii)` as `<h3>` / `<h4>` subtitles.
3. **Theme Color Discipline**:
   - The chapter's primary `themeColor` MUST strictly be applied to:
     - All subtopics and subtitles (`(i)`, `(ii)`).
     - All bold terms ending with a colon (`:`), e.g.: `<b style="color: ${themeColor};">Shock Therapy:</b>`, `<b style="color: ${themeColor};">Panchsheel Agreement:</b>`.
     - Section badges, table headers, and question numbers (`Q1:`, `Q2:`).
   - Never leave headings or subtopics in plain white or generic browser colors.
4. **Ultra HD 3D Diagrams & Visual Flowcharts**:
   - Har zaroori topic ke liye clean visual concept card / flowchart / 3D diagram include karein.
   - **White Background Requirement:** Images / SVGs must have a crisp, high-contrast **pure white background (`#FFFDF8` or `#FFFFFF`)** inside a sleek dark card wrapper.
   - **Zero Redundant Top Titles:** Since the graphic itself contains the title boldly inside the illustration, DO NOT place duplicate `📸 Title` or `Diagram X:` on top of the card.
   - **Clean Card Container Structure**:
     ```html
     <div style="background: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.9); border: 1.5px solid rgba(${themeColor}, 0.4); border-radius: 10px; padding: 14px 16px; margin: 20px 0; box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.35); text-align: center;">
       <div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; align-items: center; background: #FFFFFF; border-radius: 8px; padding: 10px; border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.1); margin: 0 auto; max-width: 440px;">
         <!-- Ultra HD Diagram / Flowchart with White BG -->
         <img src="..." style="width: 100%; max-width: 400px; height: auto; display: block; border-radius: 6px;" alt="..." />
       </div>
       <div style="color: #CBD5E1; font-size: 14px; text-align: center; margin-top: 10px; line-height: 1.5; font-weight: 500;">
         💡 [Concise Geopolitical / Conceptual Explanation]
       </div>
     </div>
     ```
5. **Specialized High-Contrast Educational Boxes**:
   - 🧠 `thinkerQuoteBox`: Official quotes and doctrines (Nehru, Gorbachev, Deng Xiaoping, Morgenthau, Kautilya).
   - 📜 `keyTreatyBox`: Important international treaties, constitutional articles, dates, and provisions (e.g. Warsaw Pact, Shimla Agreement, Article 370, Tashkent Agreement, Kyoto Protocol).
   - ⚖️ `comparisonTableBox`: Direct side-by-side matrices (e.g. Capitalist vs Socialist Bloc, ASEAN vs European Union, Traditional vs Non-Traditional Security).
   - 💡 `boardExamTipBox`: High-scoring answer presentation guidelines for 4M and 6M board questions.
6. **Master Revision Summary at End of Overview**:
   - Conclude every chapter with a comprehensive **Key Takeaways & Chronological Timeline** summarizing crucial dates, treaties, articles, and leader names.

## 3. Tab 2: Board-Weightage Adaptive Question Bank

Questions must be strictly calibrated according to the official Board Marks Weightage so students do not face unnecessary burdens:

### Category A: 2-Marks Chapter (Chapter 10: Politics of Planned Development)
- **Total Questions:** **10 Questions**
  - **Section A: Very Short Answer (VSAT - 1 & 2 Marks) — 8 Questions (Q1 to Q8):** Essential definitions (Planning Commission, NITI Aayog, Bombay Plan, Green Revolution, 1st & 2nd Five Year Plan focus).
  - **Section B: Short Answer (SA - 2 & 3 Marks) — 2 Questions (Q9 to Q10):** Core objectives of planning, Capitalist vs Socialist model.
  - **Section C:** ❌ *None (6-Mark essays are not asked from 2-mark units).*

### Category B: 4-Marks Chapters (Chapters 6, 9, 13)
- **Total Questions:** **15 Questions**
  - **Section A: Very Short Answer (VSAT - 1 & 2 Marks) — 8 Questions (Q1 to Q8):** Crisp definitions, key events, 1-line reasons.
  - **Section B: Short Answer (SAT - 4 Marks) — 7 Questions (Q9 to Q15):** Strictly structured 4 to 5 bullet points with bold themed subheadings.
  - **Section C:** ❌ *None (6-Mark essays are not asked from 4-mark units).*

### Category C: 5, 6 & 7-Marks Chapters (Chapters 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 11, 12, 14, 15)
- **Total Questions:** **Exact 25 Questions (Golden 25 Model)**
  - **Section A: Very Short Answer (VSAT - 1 & 2 Marks) — 12 Questions (Q1 to Q12):** Definitions, treaties, dates, core concepts.
  - **Section B: Short Answer (SAT - 4 Marks) — 8 Questions (Q13 to Q20):** 80-100 words, point-wise analytical answers.
  - **Section C: Long Answer (LAT - 6 Marks) — 5 Questions (Q21 to Q25):** 120-150 words, mandatory 3-tier structure:
    1. *Introduction & Background*
    2. *Core Analysis / Dimensions* (5-6 themed headings)
    3. *Conclusion & Strategic Relevance*

## 4. Tab 3: Interactive MCQs Quiz Standards

- **15 to 20 MCQs per chapter**, 100% textbook syllabus-based.
- **Gradual 3-Tier Progression:**
  - Tier 1: Direct recall, treaties, dates, summits, key terms (Easy).
  - Tier 2: Conceptual & comparative questions (Moderate).
  - Tier 3: Assertion-Reason / Multi-statement analytical questions (Advance).
- **Interactive Feedback:**
  - Correct choice: Turns Green (`#4CAF50`) with checkmark; explanation remains hidden.
  - Wrong choice: Turns Red (`#F44336`) with cross; explanation panel reveals correct answer in **Theme Color** and concise syllabus justification in white.
